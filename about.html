About Masha Tech

Holding hands to a brighter future

Howi It All Started

Masha Tech is a small non-profit organisation based in Ebony Park, Midrand. It was stared because many young people in the area finish school without optaining the skills of using a computer, sending emails or even applying for universities or jobs.

young group of volunteers, including students and those who are done with school, decided to help by teaching these skills for free. We also help learners with homework, assigments and exam preparations.

Who Started It

Masha Tech was founded by a group of passionate young leaders from Ebony Park,who believe that eduucation is the key to braeking the cycle of poverty

Our team is made up of tutors, mentors and volunteers who give their time to help learners with homework, assignments, exam preparations and digital literacy. We are "by the youth, for the youth."

What we stand for

We believe every young person deserves access to technology and education, no matter their background



Our mission is to equip youth with the right skills so they can compete and create jobs.
