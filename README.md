## Project overview
Masha Tech is a small non-profit organization based in Ebony Park, Midrand. It was started because many young people
in the area finish school without knowing how to type, use a computer and send emails or apply for universities. A young
group of volunteers, including students, decided to help close this gap by teaching digital skills for free and also help learners
with exam prep and homework.

## website goals and object
The main goal of the website is to build trust and make it easy for people to get involved. A well build website is not
just nice to have, it directly affects whether Masha Tech grow.

## key features
•	Homepage: a logo, a short quote and a navigator.
•	About Us: how Masha Tech started, what it stands for, who runs it and what we offer.
•	Services: images of the kind of support and services we offer and details about the days and times and times we are available.
•	Enquiry page: a form where people can ask about joining, volunteering, or sponsoring.
•	Contact page: contact details, a map showing our organization location, and a contact form.
